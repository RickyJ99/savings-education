Recovery time & maximum drawdown

How long it took to get back to the pre-crisis peak — and how deep the hole was.

The most important number isn't the drawdown — it's the recovery time. A 100% stock portfolio crashes harder, but history shows it also recovers to new highs. The danger is not the crash: it's selling during the crash and locking in the loss.
Bonus: when bonds fail — 2022
Most people think bonds are always "safe." 2022 proved otherwise.
2022 rate shock: As central banks raised interest rates at the fastest pace in 40 years, global government bonds lost ~16% in a single year — their worst performance since the 1940s. A diversified portfolio (70/30 or 50/50) cushioned the blow because stocks recovered faster than bonds in 2023. No asset class is permanently safe.
